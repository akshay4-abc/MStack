Technology in Everyday Life

By Lijith Lal

Technology has become an essential part of modern life, making communication, education, and work more efficient than ever before. People use smartphones, computers, and the internet every day to stay connected and access information. While technology offers many benefits, it should always be used responsibly to maintain a healthy balance between the digital and real world.

Technology is best when it brings people together.
